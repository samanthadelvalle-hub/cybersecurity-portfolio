# Samantha DelValle | Cybersecurity Portfolio

## About Me

More than 10 years in apparel operations built a habit of checking the details, tracing problems to their source, and following fixes through to resolution. Managing data discrepancies, system issues, and process exceptions required understanding what went wrong and how to prevent it from happening again. That foundation now shapes an approach to cybersecurity that connects technical findings with business risks and practical improvements.

Current studies toward a BS in Cybersecurity at Southern New Hampshire University combine coursework, guided labs, and personal projects, with graduation expected in May 2027. This work develops skills in securing systems, investigating suspicious activity, and evaluating risks and controls. Career interests span technical security and governance, risk, and compliance.

The value of a project is in understanding why something worked, why it failed, and what the evidence actually shows. Project write ups explain the decisions behind the work, unexpected results, and troubleshooting steps. Screenshots and supporting files help readers follow the process, while reflections identify lessons learned and what could be approached differently next time.
